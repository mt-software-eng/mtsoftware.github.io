Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026  ·  MT Software  ·  MT Planner

MT Planner is built on a simple principle: your plans are yours. MT Software does not operate servers that receive, store, or process your app data.

1. Data Storage

The tasks you write in MT Planner — their text, the day they belong to, and whether they are done — are stored on your device. On iPhone, iPad, and Mac, if you are signed in to iCloud, your tasks sync between your devices through your personal Apple iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit infrastructure.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

MT Software does not collect, store, sell, or have access to your personal data or your tasks. We have no servers that receive your MT Planner data. iCloud sync is handled by Apple under your Apple Account and iCloud settings.

3. Accounts

MT Planner has no accounts and no sign-in. The app does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information.

4. Deleting Your Data

When you delete a task, MT Planner keeps a small deletion marker so that the deletion also reaches your other devices. Removing the app deletes the data stored on that device. Data synced through iCloud can be managed or removed from your iCloud storage settings.

5. Analytics

MT Planner does not include any analytics, crash reporting, tracking, or advertising SDKs. We have no visibility into how you use the app.

6. Children's Privacy

MT Planner does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.

8. Contact

Questions? Email us at support@mtsoftware.app.
